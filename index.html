How to use this calendar

Tap any expense to Edit its amount, name, date, or category. Tap + Add expense to record an Uber, restaurant, coffee, or surprise bill. Choose Chase payment, PNC payment, or Savings transfer when you want those balances to update too. Paycheck carryover updates instantly when you save.

Set starting bank and credit card balances at the top. For new credit card purchases, add a Credit card charge and select the card; mark it posted when it appears on your statement. For Capital One payments, add a Capital One payment (do not mark the flight reserve as a second payment). Set starting bank balances at the top, then mark expenses Paid and paychecks Received to update the live balances. Savings transfers move money from checking to savings. Capital One reserves are planning entries and do not affect live checking; mark the actual card payment paid instead. You can reset your starting balances to match your bank app at any time. Changes are saved in this browser on this device. Use Export backup to save your data as a JSON file and Import backup to restore it. If you open the calendar on another device, import your backup there. The page works offline after downloading, but browser data may be lost if site storage is cleared.

Budget note: Capital One reserve entries represent money set aside for charges, not extra cash paid twice. Actual statement due dates and balances should be checked separately. Chase payoff estimates assume no new charges, fees, or interest. The plan includes February and March-rent allocations beyond paycheck 8 so you can keep tracking.

Set current credit card balances

Enter the balances currently shown in your card apps. This takes a snapshot of transactions already marked paid/posted, so they are not counted again. New marked Chase/PNC/Capital One payments decrease the appropriate card balance. Card charges increase it when marked posted. No automatic bank connection.

Update current bank balances

Enter the amounts currently shown in your bank app. This resets the ledger starting point, so transactions already marked paid will not be subtracted again. Afterward, mark new transactions paid and paychecks received. No bank connection required.
